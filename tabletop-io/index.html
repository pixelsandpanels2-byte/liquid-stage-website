ABOUT TABLETOP iO

Visibility while
the operation is still live.

Tabletop iO is an inventory and operations intelligence platform designed for live, high-density environments.

It gives operational teams a current view of stock, sales and execution across counters, stores and teams, while working alongside the point-of-sale and billing systems already in use.

WHAT TABLETOP iO BRINGS

Clearer operations.
While they are happening.

01

Live Stock Visibility

See stock across counters, stores and movements in one operational view.

02

Variance Intelligence

Compare sales with stock movement so discrepancies can surface while the event is still live.

03

Structured Workflows

Allocation, restocking and closing follow defined, accountable operational workflows.

04

Same-Night Reconciliation

Bring closing and reconciliation into the same operational cycle instead of waiting days.

05

Offline-First Reliability

Operations can continue in environments where internet connectivity is unpredictable.

06

Operational Reporting

Access structured reporting that helps teams review stock movement, sales alignment and event-level performance more clearly.

For Liquid Stage, Tabletop iO supports the operational discipline behind complex, high-volume event environments.
