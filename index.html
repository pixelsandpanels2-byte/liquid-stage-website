THE LIQUID STAGE PROCESS

From brief
to final pour.

Great bar experiences start long before the first drink is served. Our process connects creative hospitality with operational planning so that every counter, bartender, beverage and backend system is ready when the guests arrive.

01

Understand

The event comes first. We understand the venue, audience, format, guest count and experience you want to create.

02

Design

Build the right beverage experience. Our team develops the service format, bar zones, beverage programme, signature cocktails and guest journey.

03

Plan

Put the operation behind it. Manpower, inventory, equipment, chilling, logistics and replenishment are mapped before service begins.

04

Execute

Bring the bar to life. Our bartenders, supervisors and hospitality teams manage setup and service on ground.

05

Monitor

Stay ahead of demand. Inventory movement, consumption and replenishment are monitored during large-scale operations.
