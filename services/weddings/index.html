Wedding Bar FAQs

Tailoring your wedding pour.

Does Liquid Stage provide bartenders for wedding functions?

Yes. Liquid Stage's current wedding-related offering includes professional bartending for wedding celebrations, with specific experience in flair bartending and live pouring for Sangeets and receptions.

Can you create signature cocktails for the couple?

Absolutely. We design bespoke signature cocktails that translate your story into spirits. From translating your favorite flavors into custom recipes to personalized drink names and tailored garnishes, we craft unique "His & Hers" pours that add a deeply personal touch to your celebration.

Can the bar experience change across different wedding functions?

Absolutely. Every wedding function has its own energy, and the bar should reflect it. Liquid Stage adapts every detail—from cocktail styling and glassware to the bartender's energy and service pace—ensuring the bar sets the right mood whether it’s a relaxed sundowner or a late-night reception.

Can you help with wedding beverage planning?

Yes. Liquid Stage manages end-to-end beverage planning to keep your bar running seamlessly. From calculating precise liquor quantities and sourcing mixers to handling backend logistics, we ensure flawless service for every function.

How far in advance do we need to book and finalize menus?

We recommend securing dates 2–3 months out, with tasting sessions and final menu lock-ins finalized 3–4 weeks prior to the event.

Can you create non-alcoholic or mocktail menus?

Yes. Non-alcoholic menus deserve the same artistry as signature cocktails. Liquid Stage creates craft mocktail concepts using fresh botanicals, complex flavor pairings, and tailored styling—ensuring every guest enjoys a premium drink regardless of ABV.
