Corporate Event FAQs

Before we shape the brief.

What types of corporate events does Liquid Stage provide bar services for?

Liquid Stage provides end-to-end bar and beverage management across a wide spectrum of corporate event formats, tailoring service execution, bar setups, and menus to match the specific crowd volume, guest profile, and brand objectives.

Can the cocktail menu be customised for a corporate event?

Yes, absolutely. Customizing cocktail menus for corporate events is central to what Liquid Stage offers. Rather than providing a standard plug-and-play drink list, every menu can be tailored to align with your event's specific goals, theme, and audience.

Do you offer non-alcoholic options for corporate events?

Non-alcoholic and zero-proof drink programs are a core part of Liquid Stage's corporate offerings, ensuring that every guest—regardless of drinking preference—receives a premium experience.

Do you conduct cocktail workshops for corporate teams?

Yes, Liquid Stage offers interactive cocktail and mixology workshops tailored specifically for corporate teams. These workshops are designed as engaging, hands-on experiences ideal for team building, client engagement, leadership retreats, or festive corporate gatherings.

Can Liquid Stage organise a wine tasting for a corporate event?

Yes, Liquid Stage can organize and curate wine tasting experiences tailored specifically for corporate settings. Whether designed for an intimate executive dinner, a client appreciation evening, or a dedicated breakout lounge during a conference, these sessions are structured to be both sophisticated and engaging.
