House Party FAQs

Setting your drink stage.

What does Liquid Stage's house party bartending service include?

Liquid Stage’s house party service brings a complete cocktail bar experience into your home. We provide expert mixologists, customized signature menus, and personalized drink styling, alongside total backend support—from alcohol quantity planning and mixer sourcing to inventory management and styled setups.

How does the planning process work before the event?

We handle the setup long before the first drink is served—aligning on your guest count, menu preferences, and alcohol requirements to ensure everything is locked in smoothly.

Do you handle alcohol procurement, or do we buy our own?

We provide precise quantity calculations and complete procurement lists based on your menu, so you buy exact amounts and with quantities planned precisely for your guest count and menu.

How early does the Liquid Stage team arrive for setup?

Our team arrives 60 to 90 minutes prior to your start time to prep fresh ingredients, set up the station, batch signature mixes, and chill glassware so we are ready to pour the moment your first guest arrives.

Do you offer more than standard cocktails?

Yes. Beyond classic cocktail menus, Liquid Stage offers theme-based drinks, custom "Host Pours," high-energy flair bartending, and full sensory menu styling tailored to the aesthetic and vibe of your party.
